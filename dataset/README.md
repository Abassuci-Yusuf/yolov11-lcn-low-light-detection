The dataset was collected as part of a research project at Universitas Syiah Kuala, Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

- with a total images use : 2,000
- classes : 7 (car, mototrycle, bus, motorized-trishaws, truck, person, traffic sign)
- conditions : low-light illumination and mixed traffic
- location : Banda Aceh, Indonesia

The dataset is not publicly available as it belongs to the university research group. For inquiries regarding access, please contact Universitas Syiah Kuala.
